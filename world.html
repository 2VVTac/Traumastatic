The World of Traumastatic

Welcome to the universe of my project Traumastatic (which translates to "Static Traumas"). This world is divided into three species: demons, angels, and humanoids (which also include dolls and robots).

Humanoids are completely unaware of the existence of angels and demons. When they die, their bodies decompose just like humans, without them ever knowing that their souls end up in either Heaven or Hell.

★ Calm Town

A quiet and peaceful town. Because angels and demons often wander around here, the local police—the Black and White Police Department—have to patrol and check the area frequently.

★ Empty Town

An abandoned town that used to be densely populated just five years ago. Nowadays, people occasionally use it as a dumping ground for unwanted junk.

★ Village of Rag Mechanics

A settlement inhabited by rag mechanics—creatures of short stature (usually around 140 to 160 cm tall). They are mostly hired as workers and engineers to build machinery and various tech.

★ Fashion Town

Just as densely populated as Calm Town, though much more stylish and lively. Most of its residents are mannequins ranging from 170 cm to 4 meters in height.

★ Town of Sleeping Toys

A town inhabited by plush toys. They spend most of their time sleeping—even during the day. When they are awake, they enjoy simple, childish activities like having tea parties, playing house, and other innocent games.
